Emotion check-in, third draft

Every check-in now asks one more honest question: am I doing what I should be doing, or am I escaping it? The night and weekly reviews then show what you avoid and which feelings come right before you escape. Example data below.

What changed after your feedback

  • Should vs actuallyEach check-in logs what I feel, what I should be doing, and what I'm actually doing. The last tap is the honest one: On it or Escaping.
  • New streak ruleA day counts when you check in during 5 different hours and close it with the night review. Missing a day resets the streak to zero.
  • Avoidance patternsThe night review shows what you escaped from, where you went, and what you felt just before.
  • Weekly reviewSunday night, after the night review. Five minutes on the week's patterns and one change for next week.

Home-screen web app or installed app?

Recommendation: the installed app. Reminders and quick lock-screen answers are the heart of this app, and a web app can only get them through a server, with known silent failures. Checked against Apple and WebKit documentation, November 2026.

Home-screen web appInstalled app (built with Xcode)
Reminders every N minutesNeeds a server. iPhone web apps can't schedule notifications or run timers while closed, so a server must push every reminder.The phone schedules them itself. No server. iOS holds up to 64 pending reminders, about 2.5 days at 30 minutes, refilled each time you open the app.
Answering from the lock screenNot possible. No buttons and no typed replies. Tapping opens the app.On it, Escaping, and typed replies right from the notification, even while locked.
ReliabilityKnown iOS problems: push subscriptions can stop working quietly, and Apple's push service may still report success. Some people report there's no sound option.Local notifications run on the phone and don't depend on a network or server.
PrivacyEntries stay on the phone, but the server knows your reminder schedule and holds a push address.Nothing leaves the phone.
Keeping your dataSafe if you use it daily, since home-screen apps are exempt from Safari's 7-day storage wipe. Storage is still "best effort", so export matters.Stored like any app's data. Kept until you delete the app.
ExtrasA badge count on the icon. No widgets.Lock-screen widget later. Breaking through Focus mode needs the paid account.
SetupNo Xcode. You host the page and run a small server, such as a free Cloudflare Worker.Update macOS to 26.6 or later, install Xcode, and turn on Developer Mode on the iPhone.
Staying installedStays installed. Updates show up the next time you open it.Free Apple ID: stops opening after 7 days until you reinstall from the Mac. $99 a year: a development install lasts about a year, or use TestFlight with a new build every 90 days.
CostFree on free hosting tiers.Free with weekly reinstalls, or $99 a year.
Other devicesAlso opens on your Mac or any browser.iPhone only, unless built for Mac too.

Decisions still open

Should rest count as escaping?

Lunch or a walk you planned isn't escaping. Without a separate option, the patterns get muddied.

  1. Add a small "Planned break" option. It doesn't count as escaping and doesn't show in avoidance patterns.Recommended
  2. Only On it or Escaping. Breaks get logged as escaping.

Does a pause protect the streak?

Missing a day breaks the streak. Pause until tomorrow exists for sick days and travel.

  1. A paused day breaks the streak like any missed day. This is the strict version of your rule.Recommended
  2. A paused day neither counts nor breaks. Limit it to a few days a month so it can't become a loophole.
